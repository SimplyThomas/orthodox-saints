# Saints to add — recent canonizations (2022–2025)

Checked 2026-08-04 against `data/saints.csv`. This came out of the Witnesses review: while
verifying that no proposed "witness" had actually been glorified, it became clear the
Church has canonized a great many people in the last three years and the data has caught
only some of them.

**25 saints to add**, plus 2 needing research. The good news first: **the whole Romanian
2024 batch of 16 is already in** (OS-2616…OS-2631), so this is a smaller job than it looks.

Every feast day below is quoted from the canonizing Church's own decree or its official
news service. Nothing here has clergy review (§9), and none of these rows exist yet — this
is a work list, not data.

---

## B. Serbian Orthodox Church — Holy Assembly of Bishops, May 2025 → **8 to add**

⚠️ **Read the calendar note before writing these.** The Serbian decree prints dates as
**civil/liturgical** ("October 4/September 21"). This data stores the **liturgical**
number so the Old/New toggle can do its own arithmetic — so the **second** number is what
goes in `Feast Day(s)`. Taking the first would shift every one of them by thirteen days.

| Saint | Lived | Decree | **Feast to record** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nikodim (Milaš), Bishop of Dalmatia** — canonist, author of *Orthodox Church Law* | 1845–1915 | Oct 4 / Sep 21 | **Sep 21** |
| **Protosyncellus Kiril (Cvjetković)**, Confessor of Bezdin and Dalmatia-Boka — 25 years imprisoned and exiled resisting Uniatism | 1791–1857 | Oct 12 / Sep 29 | **Sep 29** |
| **Priest Mihailo Barbić**, parish priest of Krtole | — | Jan 22 / 9 | **Jan 9** |
| **Priest Ilija Rodić** | — | May 9 / Apr 26 | **Apr 26** |
| **Venerable Martyr Jovan of Stjenik** | — | Aug 10 / Jul 28 | **Jul 28** |
| **Blessed Ana**, mother of St Vasilije of Ostrog | — | May 13 / Apr 30 | **Apr 30** |
| **Martyrs of Reškovice**, killed by the Turks in 1688 | †1688 | May 4 / Apr 21 | **Apr 21** |
| **Martyrs of Garavice and of Bihać-Petrovac** | 20th c. | Jul 27 / 14 | **Jul 14** |

**Already present:** the **Martyrs of Rmanj** (**OS-0372**).

⚠️ **But check OS-0372's feast.** Ours reads **Sep 10**; the decree reads "September 10 /
August 28", which by the rule above makes the liturgical date **Aug 28**. Either our row
took the civil date, or the source it came from gave Sep 10 outright. Worth resolving —
and if it is wrong, the same slip may sit on other Serbian rows.

The decree also **moved** an existing commemoration: the **Child Martyrs of Jastrebarsko
and Sisak** are now kept on **Jul 13 / Jun 30**. If we hold that row, its date needs
updating.

## D. Needs research before it can be listed properly

- **Patriarchate of Alexandria, 2025** — canonized a **St Silvester** and a **St
  Chrysostomos**. Neither full name nor feast day established yet; both are common names,
  so this needs the actual decree rather than a search result.
- The Serbian communique names **six individuals and three groups**; the eight above plus
  Rmanj account for nine entries, which fits — but the primary text should be read once
  more before anyone treats section B as complete.

## Suggested order of work

1. **Section C first** — three saints, high English-language readership, and Gavrilia is
   already being asked for.
2. **Section A** — fourteen rows in one coherent batch, all with confirmed dates, and it
   materially improves the data's coverage of Orthodox women.
3. **Section B** — do the OS-0372 date question first, since the answer decides how to
   write the other eight.
4. **Section D** — only after someone has the Alexandrian decree in hand.

All of these are `Era: Modern` / `Post-Byzantine` with real biographies available, so they
are honest full rows rather than stubs — with the exception of Platonida of Argeș (15th c.)
and Mavra of Ceahlău (17th–18th c.), where a stub may be the honest outcome.
