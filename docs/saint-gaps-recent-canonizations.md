# Saints to add — recent canonizations (2022–2025)

Checked 2026-08-04 against `data/saints.csv`. This came out of the Witnesses review: while
verifying that no proposed "witness" had actually been glorified, it became clear the
Church has canonized a great many people in the last three years and the data has caught
only some of them.

**STATUS: worked through 2026-08-04 — all 25 are in, plus the one saint section D was
missing (OS-3018…OS-3043).** What remains is listed under "Still open" at the foot.
Nothing here has clergy review (§9). Every feast day below is quoted from the canonizing
Church's own decree or its official news service; where the decree prints a
civil/liturgical pair, the entry records the **liturgical** number (see §B).

### The OS-0372 question — answered, and it was wrong

The decree gives the **Martyrs of Rmanj** as "September 10 / August 28", so the liturgical
number is **Aug 28**; our row carried the civil date. **OS-0372 is corrected** (and its
rank set to Venerable-Martyr; Martyr, the decree's own wording).

**One other row had the same slip: OS-0846, Nikolai of Zhicha.** It read Mar 18, which is
the civil date; the Serbian church calendar keeps his repose on **Mar 5** and the
translation of his relics on **Apr 20** (May 3 civil). Both are now recorded. The rest of
the modern Serbian rows were spot-checked against the church calendar and read correctly.

The decree also **moved** the **Child Martyrs of Jastrebarsko and Sisak** to
**Jul 13 / Jun 30**. We hold no row for them — a gap, not a stale date.

number the Church keeps.)

(**OS-2598**), both canonized 2023.

## D. Patriarchate of Alexandria, 2025 — **resolved**

The Holy Synod of Alexandria canonized two saints on **9 October 2025**:

- **Sylvester, Pope and Patriarch of Alexandria** (Cretan; patriarch 1569–1590), feast
  **Feb 19** — **added as OS-3043**, with a documented-distinct note against St Sylvester,
  Pope of Rome (OS-0376). First liturgically commemorated 19 February 2026.
- **Chrysostomos Papasarantopoulos** (1903–1972), founder of the Orthodox mission in the
  Congo, feast **Dec 29** — **already present as OS-2744**.
