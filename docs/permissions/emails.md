# Permission correspondence — templates

Wording for the three emails this program needs. All of them point at
[`AGREEMENT.md`](AGREEMENT.md) rather than restating its terms, so there is one
place where the terms live and one place to change them.

Keep them short. Both grants so far were given by people who read a few paragraphs
and said yes; a long email invites a long silence.

---

## 1. Asking a new publisher

Send to the shop's contact form or their published address. Subject:
**Permission to show your icons on a free Orthodox saints reference site**

> Dear [name],
>
> I run **A Cloud of Witnesses** (orthodoxsaintfinder.com), a free, non-commercial
> reference site that helps catechumens and parishioners find and read about
> Orthodox saints. It carries no advertising, sells nothing, and has no paywall.
>
> I would like to ask permission to show images of icons from your catalogue on the
> relevant saints' pages. Each image would carry a visible credit naming you and a
> link back to that icon's own page on your site, so a reader who wants the icon
> is sent to you to buy it.
>
> I have written out exactly what that means — what we do with an image, what we
> never do with it, and how to make us remove everything with a single email:
>
> [link to AGREEMENT.md]
>
> In short: we host a small resized copy (never hotlinking your server), we never
> sell, redistribute, or offer your images for download, we never claim ownership,
> and a reply from you asking us to stop takes effect within two business days.
>
> Two publishers have already granted this on the same terms — Theophany Works and
> Legacy Icons — and I am glad to point you at a live page so you can see how their
> icons are credited before deciding.
>
> A simple "yes" by reply is all that is needed; if you would prefer something
> signed, I will gladly produce it.
>
> With thanks,
> [name]

**Notes.** Offer the live sample page early — Theophany Works asked for one before
granting, and seeing a real credited page is what turned a conditional yes into a
full one. Never send a formal-looking contract first.

## 2. Confirming a grant already given

For Theophany Works and Legacy Icons, whose permission was given informally by
email before this document existed. The point is to **write down what we understood
and invite a correction** — not to reopen the question. Subject:
**Your icons on orthodoxsaintfinder.com — a short record of our arrangement**

> Dear [name],
>
> Thank you again for permitting us to show [publisher]'s icons on
> orthodoxsaintfinder.com. The site launched to our parish in July and your icons
> are on it now.
>
> As the number of publishers has grown I have written down the terms properly, so
> that what you agreed to is on record rather than in an inbox:
>
> [link to AGREEMENT.md]
>
> Nothing in it is new — it is the arrangement we already have, set out plainly:
> your images are hosted by us at small size, credited to you on every page they
> appear on, and linked back to that icon's own page on your site. We do not sell,
> redistribute, or offer them for download. One email from you removes all of them
> within two business days.
>
> Where things stand today: **[N] of your images appear across [N] pages** —
> [breakdown]. You can see them at [link].
>
> If any of that differs from what you understood, or if you would like the credit
> worded differently, a particular image removed, or the links pointed somewhere
> else, just say so and I will change it. If it reads correctly, no reply is
> needed.
>
> With gratitude,
> [name]

**Current figures for the two existing grants** (as of 2026-08-07 — regenerate
before sending; see [`README.md`](README.md#counting-what-is-in-use)):

- **Theophany Works** — 268 images, 282 placements: 120 saint portraits, 121 saint
  carousel cards, 13 feast portraits, 19 feast cards, 4 angel portraits, 5 angel
  cards.
- **Legacy Icons** — 254 images, 267 placements: 153 saint portraits, 4 saint
  carousel cards, 18 feast portraits, 74 feast cards, 2 angel portraits, 16 angel
  cards.

**Legacy Icons has an open question** worth folding into their note: they asked
whether we would route outbound links through tracked or affiliate URLs. Answer it
plainly — we are happy to, it costs us nothing, and §6 of the agreement already
offers it.

## 3. Acknowledging a revocation

Send **after** the removal is done, not before — the confirmation should be true
when it arrives. Subject: **Your icons have been removed from
orthodoxsaintfinder.com**

> Dear [name],
>
> Thank you for letting me know. [Publisher]'s images have been removed from
> orthodoxsaintfinder.com — [N] images across [N] pages, taken down on [date] and
> deleted from our servers. The pages now show a plain monogram in their place.
>
> Nothing of yours is retained in our data, our exports, or our published files. I
> am sorry to lose them; the offer stands if you ever wish to revisit it, and there
> is no need to explain.
>
> With thanks for the time they were shown,
> [name]

If instead the request was for **specific images** rather than the whole grant, say
which ones came down and confirm the rest remain — and record the restriction in the
publisher's file under `docs/permissions/` so it survives the conversation.
