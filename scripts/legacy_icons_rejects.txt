# Legacy Icons — product matches a human has REJECTED.
#
# One product URL per line. scripts/download_legacy_icons.py marks any listed
# product `decision=skip, confidence=rejected` and clears its guessed target, so
# a wrong match stays rejected across re-harvests.
#
# This file exists because rejections kept coming back. The review queue lives
# in git-ignored dist/, so re-running the harvest rebuilt it from scratch and
# re-proposed matches that had already been examined and thrown out — "Warrior
# Saints" and "Eugene of Sebaste" were both re-proposed for a second batch
# within an hour of being rejected from the first. A judgment call that is not
# written down is a judgment call you make again.
#
# Rejecting here says only "not this row." Several of these are perfectly good
# icons that simply need a different destination, and a couple point at saints
# missing from the data entirely (see docs/data-gaps-from-legacy-icons.md).

# --- Generic titles that name nobody ---
# "Warrior Saints (XVIIc)" had matched Peresvet & Oslyabya (OS-0290). Neither
# the title nor the alt text names a single saint.
https://legacyicons.com/warrior-saints-xviic-icon-s371/

# --- One member of a multi-saint row, which the row is not about ---
# Solo Eugene onto OS-2341, the FIVE-martyr row of Eustratius, Auxentius,
# Eugene, Mardarius and Orestes. Eustratius is the principal, not Eugene.
https://legacyicons.com/saint-eugene-of-sebaste-xxc-icon-s477/
# Solo Martha onto OS-1280, "Martha AND Mary, the Sisters of Lazarus".
https://legacyicons.com/saint-martha-the-sister-of-lazarus-icon-s497/
#
# (Contrast: Cecilia S578 -> OS-2231 "Cecilia of Rome and those with her", and
# Clement S299 -> OS-0518 "Clement of Ancyra and Martyr Agathangelus", are both
# ACCEPTED. The named saint is the principal of the row and heads its title.)

# --- A many-saint icon onto a single-saint row ---
# Four saints (Peter, Paul, Olga, Xenia) onto OS-0147, one Xenia.
https://legacyicons.com/saints-peter-paul-olga-and-xenia-icon-s384/

# --- Different saint of the same name ---
# Edward the CONFESSOR onto OS-0171, Edward the MARTYR (+978). Two different
# kings. The Confessor died in 1066 — post-schism Western, out of scope (§1) —
# so there is no row for him and there should not be one.
https://legacyicons.com/saint-edward-the-confessor-king-of-england-icon-s467/
#
# Hilda of Whitby onto OS-0167, Hereswith of Northumbria — her SISTER. The
# match came off Hereswith's Also Known As, which literally reads "sister of
# St. Hilda of Whitby". Hilda is missing from saints.csv; recorded as a gap.
https://legacyicons.com/saint-hilda-of-whitby-whirledge-icon-s582/

# --- Wrong database ---
# The two ARCHANGELS onto OS-0808, Michael the New Martyr. This is a hosts
# record (HH-####), not a saint, and belongs in host_images.csv.
https://legacyicons.com/st-michael-and-st-gabriel-clark-icon-set-y030/

# --- A specific commemoration matched onto a GENERIC feast row ---
# "All Saints of Mount Athos" onto FF-0064, the Sunday of All Saints of the
# LOCAL Church. That Sunday is whichever local church you are in; wiring an
# Athonite synaxis as its icon would quietly make the site answer "Athos".
https://legacyicons.com/all-saints-of-mount-athos-xixc-icon-f270/

# --- Right saint, wrong episode ---
# "Saint Theodore and the Princess" onto FF-0033, the Saturday of St Theodore
# the Recruit. That Saturday commemorates the miracle of the kollyva; this is
# the dragon-and-princess legend. Same saint, a different story, and the feast
# icon has to be of the feast.
https://legacyicons.com/saint-theodore-and-the-princess-icon-f346/

# --- A composite of MANY feasts onto ONE feast ---
# The "Great Feasts" icons are twelve-panel composites of the whole festal year.
# They matched FF-0001 (Pascha) because the Resurrection sits at their centre,
# but they would sit just as well on any of the twelve — which is the argument
# against filing them under one. If the site ever wants a festal-year image,
# it needs a home of its own, not a card on Pascha.
https://legacyicons.com/great-feasts-xxic-icon-f223/
https://legacyicons.com/great-feasts-xviiic-icon-f208/

# --- A saint's PORTRAIT onto that saint's FEAST ---
# A Deesis portrait of the Forerunner onto FF-0020, the Beheading. Same person,
# but a feast's carousel should show the feast; the portrait belongs on his
# saint row. Same rule that rejected "Theodore and the Princess" above.
https://legacyicons.com/saint-john-the-baptist-deesis-icon-s432/
